ABOUT

Luke Bertoz is a music producer and composer from Northern Rivers, NSW, Australia. From a young age, he developed a passion for music and began playing piano. He later went on to study Audio Engineering at the SAE Institute in Sydney and then furthered his education by studying screen music at the Australian Film Television and Radio School (AFTRS).

Luke also rocks out in his own music project called Tales in Space, which won the John Lennon Songwriting Contest and was a finalist in the Vanda & Young Songwriting Contest. In addition to his work with the band, Luke also composes music for TV and has had his work featured in commercials for Lite n' Easy, Real Insurance, HCF, and many more. He is known for his versatility and ability to create unique and catchy music that resonates with audiences.
